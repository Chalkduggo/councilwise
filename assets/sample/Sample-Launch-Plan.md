# Brightline Notes — 2027 Launch Plan (sample document)

*This is a fictional sample document that ships with Councilwise so you can try a council without your own file. Brightline Notes is an invented company.*

## 1. Executive summary
Brightline Notes is a meeting-notes app for small agencies. We plan to launch publicly in Q2 2027, reach 1,200 paying teams by the end of 2027 and break even in month 14. The launch includes the AI summary feature, calendar sync and a team workspace.

## 2. Market
Agencies with 5–50 people spend about four hours a week writing up client meetings. Three customer interviews confirmed the pain. We estimate the market at $2.1 billion a year.

## 3. Goals
- Strong adoption among agencies in the first year.
- 1,200 paying teams by December 2027.
- Net revenue retention above 110%.

## 4. Product
Phase one: AI summaries, action items, calendar sync. Phase two (2028): team workspace, CRM integrations. The summary feature uses a third-party video and speech API.

## 5. Pricing
$19 per user per month, billed annually. The main competitor charges $49 per user per month, so we are clearly cheaper.

## 6. Go-to-market and revenue
- Funnel: 40,000 website visitors a month, 2% sign up for a trial, 15% of trials convert.
- Forecast: 100 teams in month 1, growing 25% a month.
- Average team size: 6 users.

## 7. Budget
| Item | 2027 |
|---|---|
| Engineering (4 people) | $560,000 |
| Marketing | $240,000 |
| Cloud (storage at $0.12 per GB) | $48,000 |
| Support (1 person) | $70,000 |
| Total | $918,000 |

## 8. Team and capacity
Four engineers, one designer, one support person. Each engineer delivers about 120 hours of feature work a month. The month-9 plan needs 680 engineering hours.

## 9. Milestones
1. Private beta — January 2027
2. Pricing page live — February 2027
3. Calendar sync — March 2027
4. Integration with the speech API — April 2027 (owner to be decided)
5. Public launch — Q3 2027
6. 500 paying teams — September 2027
7. Security review — two weeks after launch

## 10. Technology
Cloud-hosted web app. Speech-to-text through a third-party API that the vendor says will be retired in mid-2027; we will review alternatives later.

## 11. Compliance
We will comply with data-protection law, including the new regulation that takes effect on 1 January 2027. Details to follow.

## 12. Risks
- Competitors copy the feature.
- Slower sign-ups than planned.
