# Code of conduct

Councilwise is a friendly, welcoming project. Everyone who takes part — in issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere else — is expected to:

- Be respectful and patient, especially with newcomers.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully. Critique ideas, not people.
- Avoid harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, and sharing other people's private information.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits or issues that break these rules, and may block repeat offenders.

To report a problem, open a private security advisory or contact the maintainer through the email on their GitHub profile.
